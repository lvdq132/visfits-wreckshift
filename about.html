Break the city.
Rewrite the moment.

Visfits: Wreckshift is a five-district demolition puzzle with one chance to change time. Clear coral towers within the push budget. Your one rescue permits a redirect even at the push limit; every launch still counts toward the total. Keep every mint landmark standing. If a chain goes wrong, rewind the collapse, hold a moment, and redirect a tower to change what happens next.

Three moves to learn

  1. Start a chain. Select a coral tower, aim away from mint landmarks, and push.
  2. Catch the mistake. Rewind during a collapse or after a failed move. Each attempt gives you one rescue.
  3. Change the outcome. With time held, select a standing coral tower and push in a new direction. The city resumes from that instant.

Playing in your browser

Choose a tower, direction, and strength, then press Make It Fall. Rewind time opens the timeline; move the slider to choose an instant. Redirect with a new push, or choose Return to present. A/D aim, Space pushes, Z rewinds, and R rebuilds. The guided rescue practice lets you rehearse a mistake and its correction. Sound can be muted at any time.

Playing on Meta Quest

Open this site's HTTPS address in Quest Browser and choose Enter VR. Point and pinch to select a tower or spatial button. Grab the orange handle, pull opposite the intended fall, and release. Pull distance selects Soft, Firm, or Hard strength; a brief tap does not launch.

The blue Time Spool controls time. Pull it toward you to wind the recorded collapse backward, then release to hold the selected instant. Choose a standing coral tower and use the orange handle to redirect. Merely picking up the spool without a deliberate draw does not start a rescue. Spatial controls also provide navigation, rebuilding, and exit.

The miniature is fixed in the virtual world. This build does not scan your room, use passthrough, or recenter. Choose a clear, comfortable play area using your headset's normal boundary setup.

Rescue first. Then master it.

A successful rescue earns Saved in time. Clear the district at or below its par without rewinding to earn Perfect shift. Committing a selected instant spends the rescue, even if you then return to the present. An interrupted native pull before commit costs nothing. Rebuild resets the district and gives you a fresh rescue. Your best score and earned mastery are retained when browser storage is available.

Privacy and saved progress

The game has no account, analytics integration, advertising, backend, or camera/microphone recording. Best district scores, mastery, and the best run's rewind count are stored locally in this browser, alongside earlier clearance ratings. Clearing this site's browser data removes them. The game still works if storage is blocked or contains a malformed record; persistence may be unavailable.

The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests. IWSDK may request hand/controller models from SDK-hosted URLs; those hosts receive normal network metadata. During an immersive session, WebXR poses and hand inputs control the game locally. The short physics history used for rewind stays in memory and is not uploaded.
