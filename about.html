Small city.
Big consequences.

Visfits: Wreckshift is a five-district precision-demolition game. Clear the coral towers within the push budget. Keep every mint landmark standing. The first building receives your shove; collisions decide what follows.

Playing in your browser

Select a coral tower, choose direction and strength, then press Make It Fall. A/D aim, Space pushes, and R rebuilds. Complete a district to move on, or use the district selectors. Sound can be muted.

Playing on Meta Quest

Open this site's HTTPS address in Quest Browser and choose Enter VR. Point and pinch to select a tower or a spatial button. Grab the orange handle, draw it back opposite the intended shove, then release. Pull distance chooses Soft, Firm, or Hard strength. A short tap does not launch. Spatial controls provide aiming, push, next district, rebuild, and exit. The handle draw distance sets strength.

The miniature is fixed in the virtual world. This build does not scan your room, use passthrough, or recenter. Choose a clear, comfortable play area using your headset's normal boundary setup.

Privacy and saved progress

The game has no account, analytics integration, advertising, backend, or camera/microphone recording. District ratings are stored in this browser's local storage. Clearing this site's browser data removes them. Play still works if storage is blocked.

The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests. IWSDK may request its hand/controller models from SDK-hosted URLs; those hosts receive normal network metadata. During an immersive session, the game uses WebXR poses and hand inputs locally to control play.
