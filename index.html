    Rewrite the moment.

    1. Start a chain. Select a coral tower, aim away from mint landmarks, then push.
    2. Catch your mistake. While buildings fall or after a failed move, rewind the last collapse. You get one rescue per district. A redirect works even at the push limit; every launch still counts toward your total.
    3. Change the outcome. With time held, choose a standing tower and push in a new direction. Everything resumes from that instant.

    Special contracts: brass-banded coral towers are relays: knock them down through another tower. They cannot start a push. Solid masonry walls stay put and can catch a falling tower. Use Plan view to read gaps, and Read the district for a tactical hint.

    In VR: point and pinch to select. Pull the orange handle opposite the fall, then release. Pull the blue reel toward you to wind time back; release it, then redirect with the orange handle.

    Build a longer chain for bonus points. A Perfect shift clears at par without rewinding; saving a damaged landmark earns a separate Timekeeper bonus. Rewinding removes points from the discarded chain. Today’s remix mirrors a district into a new daily challenge, shared by everyone on the same UTC date. Your best scores stay on this device.
